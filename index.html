    About this app

    SAC tracks attendance from LKG through college. Choose School or College, pick your class or semester, then set the start date and tick "Till today" so the end date moves forward every day automatically. The Target section tells you how many days you can safely miss or need to attend. Leave reasons and all your records stay on this device only — they are never uploaded anywhere. Everything works offline; importing holidays is the only feature that needs the internet.
