    About this app

    SAC tracks college attendance across 8 semesters. Pick a semester, set the start date, and tick "Till today" so the end date moves forward every day automatically. Tap a date to mark it Working, Leave or Attended. Holidays can be single days, yearly repeats or date ranges. Everything is saved in your browser and works offline.
