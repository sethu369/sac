How SAC works

1. Set your date range

Enter the start and end date of your semester or academic year.

2. Don't know when it ends?

Turn on Till Today. SAC will calculate your attendance only up to today.

3. Mark your attendance

Tip: If you know your semester end date, enter it. If you don't, use Till Today and update the end date later.

    About

    SAC tracks attendance from LKG through postgraduate education, fully offline. Set up your education from the button at the top, then set a start and end date. The calendar always shows your full configured range. "Till today" controls only the percentage calculation: on, it counts only completed working days; off, it counts every scheduled working day in your range, including ones that haven't happened yet. All data stays on this device unless you export a backup.
